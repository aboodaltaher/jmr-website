# What Is Air Balancing — and When Does a Building Need It?

Air balancing is the measurement and adjustment of airflow through an HVAC system so that every room gets the amount of conditioned air it was designed to receive — no more, no less. It's a core part of testing and commissioning, and it's what separates a system that *runs* from one that actually *works*.

If some rooms are freezing while others are stuffy, or a new fit-out "just doesn't cool right," air balancing is usually the answer.

## The symptoms of an unbalanced system

- **Hot and cold spots** — one office or bedroom never cools while the next is arctic.
- **Rooms that feel stuffy** despite the AC running — not enough fresh or supply air reaching them.
- **Doors that slam or whistle** — a sign of pressure imbalance between spaces.
- **A new building or fit-out that underperforms** against its design.
- **A consultant or landlord requiring commissioning sign-off.**

These aren't "the AC is broken" problems — the equipment can be perfectly healthy. The air just isn't distributed correctly.

## What we actually measure

Balancing is done with calibrated instruments, not guesswork. We measure airflow at every diffuser, grille and branch, compare it against the design figures, and then adjust dampers and fan settings until each zone delivers the specified airflow. Where fresh air is involved, we also check the outdoor-air quantity against the standard for the space's use.

## The report you should insist on

A proper air balancing job ends with a documented report: measured airflow by area, a comparison against design, and the adjustments made. That report is what consultants, facility managers and landlords need for sign-off — and what gives you confidence the job was done to a standard, not by feel. [See our air balancing and airflow testing service](/air-balancing-airflow-testing-dubai).

## When you need it in Dubai

- **After installation** — commissioning a new system before handover.
- **After a fit-out or layout change** — new walls and partitions change how air should be distributed.
- **When occupants complain** of uneven temperatures or stuffiness.
- **For compliance** — when a consultant or authority requires documented airflow.
- **Indoor air quality concerns** — balancing often goes hand in hand with [fresh air and CO₂ testing](/services/ventilation-fresh-air).

## The takeaway

Air balancing turns a system that was merely installed into one that performs as designed — comfortable, efficient and documented.
