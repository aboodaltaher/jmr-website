# Why Is My AC Running But Not Cooling in Dubai?

It's the most common call we get in summer: the air conditioner is clearly running — you can hear the fan, air is coming out of the vents — but the room just won't get cold. Before anyone sells you a "gas top-up," it's worth understanding what actually causes weak cooling, because the right fix depends entirely on the cause.

We diagnose and repair this every week across Dubai, Sharjah and Abu Dhabi. Here's what's really going on, roughly in order of how often we see it.

## 1. A dirty evaporator coil

The evaporator coil is where your AC actually removes heat from the air. In Dubai's dust, that coil fouls up faster than almost anywhere — and a coated coil can't transfer heat, so the air blowing out is barely cool even though the system is working hard. This is the single most common cause of "running but not cooling," and no amount of refrigerant will fix it. The coil needs cleaning.

## 2. A blocked condensate drain

When the drain line blocks, water backs up, the coil can freeze, and cooling collapses — often with water leaking from the indoor unit as a bonus symptom. Clearing and flushing the drain restores both the cooling and stops the leak.

## 3. Low refrigerant — because of a leak

Refrigerant runs in a sealed loop. If it's genuinely low, it leaked out somewhere — a top-up without finding the leak just buys you a few weeks before you're back to square one, and you've paid twice. This is why we test for the leak and repair it, rather than selling you gas on repeat.

## 4. An oversized or undersized system

Sometimes the unit was never right for the room. An oversized AC short-cycles (cools in bursts, never dehumidifies, leaves the room clammy); an undersized one simply can't keep up on a 45°C day. This is a design problem, not a repair — and it's worth knowing before you spend on parts.

## 5. Failing compressor or fan

Less common, but when the compressor is weak or a fan motor is failing, cooling drops off and the energy bill climbs. This needs measurement to confirm, not guesswork.

## How we find the real cause

We don't quote a repair until we've measured the system — temperatures across the coil, pressures, airflow and the electrical draw. That tells us whether you need a clean, a drain clear, a leak repair or something bigger, and we put the price in writing before any work starts.
