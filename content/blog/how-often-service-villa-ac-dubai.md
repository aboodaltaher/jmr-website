# How Often Should You Service Your Villa AC in Dubai?

Short answer: **every 3 to 4 months** — three or four times a year — with the most important visits before and during the summer peak. That's more often than the "once a year" most people assume, and there's a specific reason for it in the UAE.

## Why the UAE needs more frequent servicing

In a cooler climate, an AC rests for half the year. In Dubai it runs hard for eight to ten months, often around the clock. On top of that, fine dust fouls coils and filters quickly, and high humidity keeps everything damp. A system here simply accumulates wear and dirt far faster, so the service interval has to be shorter.

A villa adds another factor: multiple units and zones, often including ducted and concealed systems that are easy to ignore because you can't see them — until airflow drops or a drain blocks.

## What happens if you stretch it

Skipping services doesn't save money, it defers a bigger bill:

- **Efficiency drops** — a neglected AC can lose 20–30% of its efficiency within two years, and that lands straight on your DEWA bill.
- **Small faults become breakdowns** — a slightly blocked drain becomes a water leak; a dirty coil becomes weak cooling in August, when everyone's booked.
- **Equipment life shortens** — systems that should last 15–20 years fail in half that.

## A realistic schedule for a Dubai villa

- **Before summer (Mar–Apr):** full service — the most important visit of the year.
- **Mid-summer (Jun–Jul):** check and clean under peak load.
- **After summer (Oct):** service to clear the season's dust.
- **Optional winter check:** for larger villas or heavy use.

Homes with pets, heavy dust exposure or allergy sufferers benefit from the more frequent end of this range.
