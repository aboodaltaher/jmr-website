# How Much Can a Smart Thermostat Save on Your DEWA Bill?

In Dubai, air conditioning is **60–70% of a typical home's summer electricity use**. So anything that makes your cooling smarter goes straight to your DEWA bill. The question we get asked most is simple: *how much will a smart thermostat actually save me?*

Here's the honest, engineer's answer — not marketing.

---

## The realistic number: 10–23%

Independent studies and manufacturer data put smart-thermostat cooling savings in the range of **10% to 23%**. Where you land depends on how wasteful your current habits are. A home that leaves the AC running at 20°C all day while everyone's out will save at the top of that range. A already-careful household will save less — but almost everyone saves something, because the thermostat optimises around the clock in ways a person never will.

On a summer DEWA bill of AED 1,500, even a **15% cooling reduction** is meaningful — and it repeats every hot month, which in the UAE is most of the year.

## Where the savings actually come from

A smart thermostat doesn't cool your home any differently — it cools it **only when it needs to**. Five features do the heavy lifting:

- **Scheduling** — automatically eases off cooling overnight and when you're at work, then has the home comfortable before you return.
- **Geofencing** — uses your phone's location to stop cooling an empty home and start again as you head back.
- **AI learning** (Google Nest) — learns your routine and builds the schedule for you, so you don't have to.
- **Eco / eco+ modes** — nudge the temperature a degree or two during expensive peak periods and high humidity, invisibly.
- **Energy reports** — show you exactly where your energy goes, so you can fix the habits that cost the most.

The single biggest saving in most Dubai homes is simply **not cooling empty rooms and empty hours** — and that's precisely what these features automate.

## Why this matters more on DEWA's tariff

DEWA uses a **progressive slab tariff** — the more you use in a month, the more each extra unit costs, up to 38 fils/kWh in the top band, plus fuel surcharge and VAT. Heavy summer cooling pushes your usage into that most expensive band. By trimming consumption, a smart thermostat can pull you *down* the slabs, so you save a little more than the raw percentage suggests. We explain the tariff mechanics in [why your AC bill is so high in Dubai](/blog/why-ac-bill-so-high-dubai).

> Note: if your building runs on **district cooling** (Empower, Emicool, Tabreed), a smart thermostat lowers the **consumption** portion of your bill, but not the fixed capacity charge. It still helps — just on one half of the bill.

## How fast does it pay for itself?

For most villas and larger apartments in Dubai, the device plus professional installation typically **pays back within one to two cooling seasons** through lower bills — and then keeps saving every year after. For a multi-zone villa using room sensors, payback is usually faster because the waste being eliminated is bigger.

## Getting the savings — installation matters

A smart thermostat only saves money if it's **configured correctly for your system and climate**. Wrong wiring, a missing C-wire, or default settings tuned for a cold country all leave savings on the table.
