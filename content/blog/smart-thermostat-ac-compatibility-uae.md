# Will a Smart Thermostat Work With My AC? A UAE Compatibility Guide

It's the first question every customer asks: *"Will a Google Nest or ecobee actually work with my air conditioner?"* The honest answer is **almost always yes — but it depends on your wiring, not your AC brand.** Here's how to tell.

---

## Compatibility is about the control system, not the logo

People assume a "Daikin thermostat" only works with Daikin. That's not how it works. Smart thermostats like Nest and ecobee talk to the **low-voltage control side** of your HVAC system, and most ducted and ducted-split systems in the UAE — regardless of brand — use a similar **24-volt control interface**.

That's why we can install smart thermostats on systems from **Daikin, Mitsubishi Electric, Mitsubishi Heavy, O General, Carrier, Trane, York, Lennox, Rheem, Goodman, Samsung, LG, Fujitsu, Hitachi, Gree, Midea, AUX, Toshiba and Bosch** — and more. The brand matters far less than the *type of control wiring* behind your existing thermostat.

## The two things that actually decide compatibility

### 1. Do you have the right control wiring (and a C-wire)?

Standard 24V systems are the easy case. The one common catch is the **C-wire** (common wire), which many smart thermostats need for continuous power. Plenty of UAE homes don't have one at the thermostat — but that's a routine fix. We run a proper C-wire or fit a compatible adapter. We explain it fully in [what is a C-wire and do you need one](/blog/what-is-a-c-wire-dubai).

### 2. Is your system 24V — or 220V / inverter / proprietary?

Some systems don't use standard 24V control:

- **220V / 240V line-voltage split systems** — common in apartments and older setups.
- **Inverter units** with their own proprietary controllers.
- **VRF / VRV and chiller fan-coil** systems with manufacturer control protocols.

These aren't "plug-and-play" for an off-the-shelf smart thermostat — and this is exactly where a lot of installers say "sorry, not possible."

## Not natively compatible? We still have a solution

Here's the important part: **even if your system isn't natively compatible, we can still make it smart.** For 220V/240V and other non-standard systems, our engineers fit a **safe interface module** that lets your Google Nest or ecobee control the AC **without modifying or damaging your machine, and without depending on or cutting into its internal PCB control board.** Your unit keeps working exactly as the manufacturer designed it — it simply gains smart control on top.
